Lesson 1 · Board awareness

The 3-Second Scan

Stop hanging pieces. Start taking free ones. One habit fixes both.

You told me your games slip away to three things: you hang pieces, you miss free material, and you run out of time. The first two are the same problem seen from two sides — nobody looked at the board through the opponent's eyes before moving. The fix is a tiny ritual you run every move.

The scan. Before you move, ask two questions:

1. Is anything of mine attacked and undefended? → save it.

2. Did my opponent leave anything undefended? → take it.

This is the Captures half of the classic Checks · Captures · Threats routine — the part that pays off fastest at your level. Full version is on the CCT reference card.

Now you try

Three positions. Read the prompt, run the scan, then click the square of the right piece. You'll know instantly if you got it.

Your tangible win this session: the two-question scan. Do it on literally every move in your next blitz game — even when it's "obvious". Slow now, automatic later.

Go deeper

Practice the loop: 5 minutes of Lichess "Hanging Piece" puzzles — same skill, instant feedback, free.
