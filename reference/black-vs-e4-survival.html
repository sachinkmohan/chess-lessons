Black vs 1.e4 — Survival Card

You play 1…d5. Here's what to do, whatever White replies.

The golden rule: never play …e6 until your light-squared bishop (c8) is already developed to f5 or g4. Bishop out, then e6.

First decide: did White take your pawn?

The 3-move survival plan

1. Resolve the centre

If White offers exd5, recapture. Otherwise leave d5 alone and develop.

Don't fixate on a "pawn fort" — fix your pieces, not your pawns.

2. Bishop out, knights before the queen

…Bf5/…Bg4 — then …e6. Develop …Nf6 and …Nc6. No early queen raids for pawns.

3. Castle

Bishop free + knight on f6 → castle kingside, connect the rooks, king safe.

Two anti-patterns to never repeat (from your own games):

  1. …e6 too early → bishop trapped on c8 (cookieboi, MarianoParisi).
  2. Greedy queen grabbing b2/free pawns → queen chased, king exposed, mated (see Lesson 4).
