# Mission: Chess — becoming a genuinely strong player

## Why
Grow from a fragile beginner into a genuinely good chess player — someone who rarely blunders,
converts won games, and understands what the position wants. Measured by a steadily climbing
chess.com Rapid rating, not by any single trick or opening.

## Success looks like
- Stop hanging pieces: a fast scan before every move is automatic (foundation — see Lesson 1).
- Convert won games into checkmate without stalemating (see Lesson 2).
- Build a large tactical pattern library through regular puzzle practice.
- Review my own losses and learn from them — the core improvement engine.
- Concrete milestones: chess.com Rapid 800, then 1200. "Great" is the direction beyond that.

## Current state (2026-06-17)
- chess.com Rapid (10 min): ~493  ← primary measure
- chess.com Blitz (3+2): ~300  (will rise as fundamentals improve; not the focus)
- Duolingo Chess "1000": an internal app score, NOT a real rating — ignore as a goal.
- Knows: Scandinavian (Black), London (White), the tactical motifs (fork/pin/skewer).

## Constraints
- Study sessions are short (5–10 min) but frequent ("Regular" commitment — most days).
- Train primarily in Rapid (10 min): enough time to think and to apply the scan.

## Out of scope
- Chasing the Duolingo score.
- New openings, deep opening theory — revisit only after ~1200.
- Advanced positional/endgame theory beyond the basics — comes at higher rungs.
