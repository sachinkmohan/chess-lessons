# Mission: Chess (chess.com blitz rating)

## Why
Climb out of the ~300 rating basement in 3+2 blitz on chess.com and feel
genuinely competent at the board, instead of losing games to one-move mistakes.

## Success looks like
- Stop hanging pieces: run a fast scan before every move so I don't leave pieces undefended.
- Take free material: notice when the opponent hands me a piece or pawn, and grab it.
- Survive the clock: a consistent, quick move-routine that also saves time in 3+2 blitz.
- Rating climbs measurably above 300 and keeps trending up.

## Constraints
- Sessions are short: 5-10 minutes of focused study at a time. Lessons must be tiny.
- Plays fast time control (3+2 blitz), so habits must be quick to execute under time pressure.
- Already knows the Scandinavian Defense (as Black) and London System (as White); also using Duolingo Chess.

## Out of scope
- Learning new openings. Openings barely affect results at this level; revisit only after ~1000.
- Deep endgame theory, complex middlegame strategy. Comes later.
