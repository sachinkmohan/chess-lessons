# Chess Resources

## Knowledge

- [Video series: "Building Habits" — GM Aman Hambleton (Chessbrah, YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL8N8j2e7RpPnpqbISqi1SJ9_wrnNU3rEm)
  THE primary source for the 400-1000 range. A GM plays from a low rating using a
  strict rule-set ("don't hang pieces, take free pieces, develop, castle"). Use for:
  building the core habits this mission is about. Watch how he scans before moving.
- [Article: "How to Stop Blundering in Chess (A Practical System)" — ChessDock](https://chessdock.com/learn/how-to-stop-blundering-in-chess)
  Clear write-up of the Checks-Captures-Threats blunder-check. Use for: the exact
  scan routine and why it works.
- [Reference: CCT / blunder-check explained — ChessWorld](https://www.chessworld.net/chessclubs/openingguide/cct-and-tactical-alertness.asp)
  Use for: deeper detail on Checks-Captures-Threats and tactical alertness.

## Skills (practice tools)

- [Lichess Puzzles (free, no ads)](https://lichess.org/training)
  3M+ real-game tactics with instant right/wrong feedback and a rating that tracks you.
  Filterable by theme (forks, pins, hanging pieces). Use for: daily tactics reps — the
  tightest feedback loop available. The single best free practice tool.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/chess (Reddit)](https://www.reddit.com/r/chess/)
  Large, active. Use for: general questions, motivation, "is this normal" sanity checks.
- [r/chessbeginners (Reddit)](https://www.reddit.com/r/chessbeginners/)
  Friendlier for low-rated players. Use for: posting a lost game and asking "where did
  I go wrong" without being talked down to.
