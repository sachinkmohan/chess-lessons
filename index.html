The Mission

Become a genuinely strong player — rarely blunder, convert won games, understand what the position wants — measured by a climbing chess.com Rapid rating. Next milestone: 800, then 1200.

Now: ~493 Rapid, ~300 Blitz. Known: Scandinavian, London, the tactical motifs. Sessions: 5–10 min, most days.
