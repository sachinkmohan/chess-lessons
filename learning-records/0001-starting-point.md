# Starting point: ~300 blitz, openings known, blunders are the bottleneck

User arrives at ~300 chess.com rating in 3+2 blitz (2026-06-16). Reports knowing the
Scandinavian (Black) and London (White) and using Duolingo Chess.

Self-reported loss causes: runs out of time, hangs pieces, misses free material. Notably
does NOT report getting mated fast. This places the zone of proximal development squarely
at **board awareness / blunder-checking**, not openings or middlegame strategy.

Implication: first lessons teach the Checks-Captures-Threats scan (starting with the
"is anything hanging?" subset, since hanging pieces + missing free material are two of the
three reported problems). Defer all opening/strategy work until blunder rate drops.
